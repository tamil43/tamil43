## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. Electricity Demand–Supply Forecasting & Energy Planning

**Domain:** Energy Analytics | Time-Series Forecasting | Machine Learning | RAG

Developing a machine learning-based decision-support system to forecast
electricity demand and supply in Tamil Nadu, analyze potential shortages
and surpluses, and support energy planning.

- Analyze historical electricity and weather-related data.
- Develop separate demand and supply forecasting models.
- Compare forecasted demand and supply to estimate the electricity gap.
- Investigate prediction uncertainty and confidence intervals.
- Integrate a RAG pipeline to generate evidence-grounded energy planning recommendations.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, TensorFlow/Keras, Machine Learning,
FAISS, RAG, Gemini API

### 2. STABILIS — Customer Revenue Analytics

**Domain:** Business Analytics | Customer Behavior | Revenue Optimization

Analyzing customer transaction data to understand revenue generation,
purchasing behavior, basket-size variability, and return-related revenue risks.

- Investigate customer purchase frequency and spending patterns.
- Analyze revenue concentration across customer segments.
- Evaluate the impact of returns on revenue and margins.
- Develop data-driven customer targeting and revenue optimization strategies.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, SQL, Data Visualization, Statistical Analysis

### 3. VoltRide — EV Ride-Hailing Analytics

**Domain:** Mobility Analytics | Revenue Analysis | Operational Optimization

Analyzed ride-hailing data to understand demand–supply imbalances,
cancellations, surge pricing, revenue leakage, and charging infrastructure needs.

- Examined cancellation patterns and operational bottlenecks.
- Analyzed weather conditions and surge multipliers.
- Evaluated potential revenue loss from cancelled rides.
- Investigated zone-level performance and charging infrastructure opportunities.
- Developed data-driven operational improvement recommendations.

**Tech Stack:** Python, Pandas, Data Analysis, Power BI
